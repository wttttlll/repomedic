# Academic Paper Writer

## Goal

Produce a structured academic paper draft
based on the user's research topic and
available evidence.

## Input

Accept:
- Research topic
- Research background (optional)
- Research methods (optional)
- Experimental results (optional)
- References (optional)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Understand the topic
Identify the research question, objectives,
scope, and expected contribution.

### Step 2: Create an outline
Follow the structure defined in
references/paper-template.md.

### Step 3: Draft the paper
Write each section in academic language.
Maintain logical consistency.

### Step 4: Handle citations
Use GB/T 7714 style.
Only cite verified or user-provided sources.
Never invent references.

### Step 5: Validate
Check:
- Section completeness
- Argument consistency
- Citation accuracy
- Unsupported claims
- Missing experimental evidence

### Step 6: Export
Produce a Markdown paper draft.
Clearly mark sections requiring additional data.

## Constraints

- Do not fabricate research results.
- Do not invent references or DOIs.
- Do not present hypotheses as proven findings.
- Preserve the user's original research intent.
